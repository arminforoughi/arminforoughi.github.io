RAX

An open robot manipulation stack: one camera on the wrist, the robot's own kinematic model, and a visual servo that measures its gains instead of guessing them. Portable across arms, and every attempt becomes a self-labelled training example. Sorts lab tubes into racks with 80% pick-up success.

Research

Cryo-EM · ML

Particle heterogeneity in cryo-EM

A machine-learning model that quantifies heterogeneity among cryo-EM particles, to pick better particles and sharpen protein structure resolution. It built new features from how each particle behaves across reconstruction iterations, and a grid search that trained thousands of model variants in parallel across a Slurm GPU cluster.

Drug safety and imaging

Searched the FDA Adverse Event Reporting System for unreported side effects of drug combinations, and contoured lesions across PET, MRI and CT scans to build an atlas of PSMA uptake after prostatectomy.
